# Technical Journal: AI Factory Gamification & Monetization Planning (v0.6.0)

**Date:** 2026-10-09  
**Scope:** Brainstorming & Planning for AGMon Gamification & Monetization  
**Topic:** Transforming AGMon into a Gamified AI Factory & Monetizable Desktop Companion  

## Context & Decision
Following user exploration of how to make AGMon a fun, addictive AI factory that generates sustainable revenue, we researched proven market leaders in the cozy developer/idle space (*Rusty's Retirement* with $3.5M+ sales, *Spirit City: Lofi Sessions* with $5.3M+ sales, and *Screeps*). We identified AGMon's unfair advantage: **Real-Work Mining**. Rather than synthetic simulation games, AGMon links XP progression directly to actual code written, tokens generated, and git commits by Claude Code, Antigravity, and OpenAI Codex.

We decided to combine **Model A (Desktop Companion & DLC Store)** and **Model C (Worker Progression & Level System)** into an official v0.6.0 roadmap.
